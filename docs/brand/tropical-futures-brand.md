## 2. Story & positioning

**Origin and name** (Project)
- Founded in 2015. The name was a deliberate critique. "Futures" answered the absence of the tropics from dominant narratives about the future. "Institute" answered the lack of institutional cultural infrastructure in tropical regions.
- There was never a grand plan. TFI has scaled up and down project by project.
- TFI speaks from Chris's hybrid, diasporic position: Chinese-Filipino, grew up in Singapore, worked in New York, with deep ties to Cebu and Manila.

**What it is, and isn't** (Project)
- "TFI is a business, not primarily an art project." It has revenue, inventory, stockists and cash flow to manage.
- The research and theory run deep, but the surface stays accessible.
- Be honest about scale: TFI is a small, independent label.
- Longer-term ambition: wider distribution across Southeast Asia and Japan. (earlier chats)

**In Chris's own newsletter words** (vault, Apr–Jun 2026)
- **Anti-scale, long haul:** "Very much anti scale, and in many ways there are some challenges on commercial viability, but at this stage in the project we're looking to keep the lights on for the long haul and pace ourselves to the end."
- **Drops, not seasons:** "…we've decided against going full seasonal and will continue to work with select retailers on specific drops across our network of stores."
- **Small runs, community first:** new product lands "here first and in the broadcast channel as we've only made a small run."

**Credentials** (Project. The vault independently shows the Singapore Biennale reel and the Art Dubai work.)

## 3. Voice

**The rules** (Project)
- Simple on the surface, theoretical and strategic underneath: "The scaffolding never shows." Think of someone knowledgeable sharing what they love with friends.
- Sounds: casual, warm, community-oriented; passionate without preaching; culturally specific, never vaguely "tropical" or exoticizing; confident, not self-important.
- Avoid:
  - Hype streetwear copy: "cop this drop", "heat check", products as status symbols, manufactured scarcity.
  - Generic AI copy: "in a world where…", "at the intersection of…", "redefining the boundaries of…". If a sentence could describe any brand, cut it.
  - "Futurism" or "tropical futurism" as decoration. In TFI's context it means something specific.
- Academic or manifesto energy is fine when grounded. Just don't lead with it.
- Flag any cultural, historical or music claim that hasn't been verified before it goes out.

**How Chris actually writes** (vault: three newsletter drafts in `inbox/`)
- Warm, casual openers: "Hello hello hello", "Hey everyone", "Ok a few things have popped up since the last email".
- Thinks out loud and lets a small aside in (the April issue opens on how fast the year is going).
- "We" for the brand, "I" for personal asides.
- Teases without hype: "That's all I'll say for now". Small runs are stated as a fact, never used as pressure.
- Thanks people by name: the friend who introduced the LA store, and Philips "for having faith in the brand".
- Low-pressure asks: "If you're interested in it, please reply this email." Rewards readers who reach the end: "If you've made it this far…" leads into a pre-order.
- Short labeled sections for updates ("New Store!", "New Release!"), exclamation marks, and "Speak soon!" as a sign-off. The April issue's title is a sports idiom: "Playing to the Whistle!"

> "Thankful for the physical footprint we have across Singapore, Manila, Kuala Lumpur, and Sydney."
>
> "It's currently only in Sydney at the moment and we're trying to figure out how to get it to other places!"
>
> "We also have our TFI Hopecore jersey available online (we do ship internationally, but the jerseys are with our mailroom in the USA)."

**Watch for:** the drafts have typos worth catching before sending: "Sneak Peak" (Peek), "friends in the are" (area), "Johhny", "in store in at".

## 6. Channels & distribution

**Webstore** — tropicalfutures.shop (Shopify). The highest-margin channel: about $25 profit per tee vs about $11–16 on consignment. A relaunch was planned but hadn't happened as of the Project's last update. (Project) Bea's creative ads brought "3 orders from 3 women" (Apr 16). The store wasn't up in mid-February, and online sales were "not doing well" on Apr 8. (vault)

**Consignment** (Project; earlier chats)
- Standard split 70/30, with TFI receiving 70%. Singapore invoices are in SGD, excluding 9% GST.
- Treated as a brand-building cost: presence in key stores in exchange for thinner margins.
- Small allocations (3–6 pieces) often mean about one unit per size, so go by what each shop asks for.
- Purveyr takes 35% instead of 30% because TFI isn't BIR-registered. Asero (30%, clean reporting) is the model partner.

## 9. How TFI makes things

**Standards Chris holds with manufacturers** (set in Dec 2025, and kept even though they cost the Storehuat relationship; see [[standards]]) (vault)
- Written SOPs from the manufacturer, a blank tech-pack template and a submission checklist.
- A service menu separating what's included from what costs extra.
- Line-itemized invoices, with e-commerce photography as its own line.
- Tech packs reviewed in-house before submission.

**Tech-pack conventions** (Russell's notes, `raw/assets/techpack-notes-russell.pdf`) (vault)
- Top-left block: order size breakdown (S/M/L/XL/XXL quantities).
- Pages for front design, back design and label placement, one for care-label placement, and a final page of graphic specs and colors.
- Keep graphics centered and symmetrical. Anchor them to points that don't change between tees (HPS = high point shoulder; the garment sides), not to stitch lines, which shift between blanks. Sample size L.
- Line drawings: dashed = stitch color, solid = panels, grey = neutral / blend into the fabric. Specify stitch colors, trims and collar details (spread, depth, fold direction).
- Blanks: use the blank's image. Custom garments: an accurate line drawing.
- Show label attachment and packaging. Send an amendment file instead of a new tech pack.

**Timing and rhythm**
- Lead time: 6–10 weeks from PO to in-hand. (earlier chats)
- TFI work mostly happens in the mornings. "Do, delegate, decide in 24 hours" applies, with Bea as the person TFI work gets delegated to ([[do-delegate-decide]]). (vault)
