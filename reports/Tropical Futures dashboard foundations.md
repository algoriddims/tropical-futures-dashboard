## Organize the dashboard around five founder decisions

The following priorities are proposed requirements, not claims about current trading conditions.

| Decision | Primary view and supporting evidence |
|---|---|
| **What needs attention today?** | A brief with confirmed cash/as-of, near-term commitments and up to three evidence-backed actions, each with due date, source and next step. |
| **What can I fulfill or allocate?** | Variant/location availability, reservations, ownership and custody; expose missing key sizes even when total product stock is positive. |
| **Who owes what, and why?** | Separate wholesale invoices and consignment settlements, including partial payments, credits, due dates and missing statements. |
| **Is the next drop ready?** | Production milestones, deposits/balances, delivery assumptions, size run, partner allocation and creative/collaborator context. |
| **What should I learn or change?** | Drop/channel performance with cost coverage, actual versus estimated margin, stock history and bounded merchandising hypotheses. |

Build the smallest ledger that preserves these distinctions: products and variants; locations and inventory movements; counterparties and versioned agreements; orders/lines; invoices/credits; consignment statements; payments and their allocations; dated costs; source imports. A retailer can have both wholesale and consignment agreements. A consignment dispatch changes custody while goods remain brand-owned; it does not itself create a sale or receipt. Shopify also distinguishes available, committed and other inventory states, so subtracting commitments again from Shopify’s available quantity can understate stock. ([Shopify inventory states](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/orders-fulfillment/inventory-management-apps/manage-quantities-states), [Consignment and ownership](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/inventory/fundamentals/understanding-inventory-management))

Keep merchandise activity, amount due to TFI and cash received distinct. Preserve agreed wholesale line prices rather than reconstructing them from today’s retail price. For consignment, show retailer consumer sales, commission, deductions, brand proceeds and payments separately. A physical stock discrepancy is unexplained until evidence identifies sale, return, transfer or loss. Shopify sales reports and payment payouts likewise represent different events; the operational dashboard should not silently choose accounting recognition policies. ([Sales definitions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/reports-and-analytics/shopify-reports/report-types/default-reports/sales-report), [Payouts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/payouts))

A compact metric contract should define open wholesale balance as invoice minus approved credits and allocated payments; open consignment settlement as verified proceeds due minus payments. Calculate drop sell-through against its identified received batch, and retailer sell-through from an explicitly covered statement period. Keep these separate from Shopify’s period sell-through convention. Unknown cost remains unknown, zero prior sales gives no comparable percentage baseline, and absent sessions prevents measured conversion. Currency, reporting timezone, event dates and completeness belong beside every total; the computer’s timezone is not automatically the business timezone. ([Shopify product analytics](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/analytics))

## Give Claude the same evidence as the screen

Claude Desktop is the recommended conversational entry point for daily questions; Claude Code handles adapters, tests and application maintenance. Expose a small MCP interface for the business brief, variant stock, open balances, drop readiness and metric explanations. Return calculated totals with source identifiers, period, currency and freshness. Persistent storage with backup/export is essential; an enterprise platform is unnecessary. A local service suits a single-computer workflow; an authenticated remote service supports broader access. Anthropic distinguishes remote connectors from desktop extensions for local resources, while Claude Code supports HTTP and local stdio MCP connections. The final deployment choice remains open. ([Connector guidance](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11725091-when-to-use-desktop-and-web-connectors), [Claude Code MCP](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/mcp))

Start with read-only imports and reconciliation, following the existing v1 scope. Later report/payment entry should validate inputs, keep audit history and prevent duplicate application. Shopify order IDs and external invoice/statement IDs need an explicit crosswalk: the same wholesale sale recorded in both systems is one event. Credentials stay outside the browser and pasted files. Imported notes supply evidence, not instructions to Claude. ([Existing v1 scope](<C:/Users/chris/OneDrive/Desktop/Kimi_Agent_Gamified Business Dashboard PRD/TFI-Terminal-PRD.md>))

Verify capabilities instead of assuming any Shopify connector supplies every metric. Standard order access covers the latest 60 days unless extended access is approved. ShopifyQL analytics has separate scope, protected-data and API-version requirements. B2B documentation inspected on the research date now lists features across Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus, with plan-dependent differences; TFI’s actual plan and enabled features remain unverified. Off-platform wholesale/consignment records must work regardless. Use versioned GraphQL imports, show incomplete history, and retain the prior valid snapshot on failure. Add webhooks only when useful, handling duplicates, ordering and periodic reconciliation. ([Order scopes](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/access-scopes), [ShopifyQL requirements](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/shopifyql/graphql-admin-api), [B2B matrix](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/getting-started/plan-features), [Webhooks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks))

## Improve through protected experiments and retained lessons

Use **Compound Engineering as the delivery process**: clarify the decision, plan, work, simplify, review and compound verified lessons. Its canonical guidance places durable non-obvious knowledge in `docs/solutions`, enforceable rules in tests/types, shared vocabulary in `CONCEPTS.md`, and local rationale near the code. A concise `CLAUDE.md` should point to these, the brand MD and metric contract. This applies the documented method; no plugin installation is claimed. ([Compound Engineering](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin), [Compounding guide](https://github.com/EveryInc/compound-engineering-plugin/blob/main/docs/guides/ce-compound.md))

Use **autoresearch as a bounded experimental pattern**: hypothesis → small change → fixed evaluation → keep or revert. Karpathy’s original project optimizes language-model training with protected evaluation and a fixed training budget. Applying that structure to dashboard work is our adaptation. Start with a batch cap of six candidates or 60 minutes, whichever comes first, and revise that budget after measuring evaluation cost. Record baseline, revision, fixture/evaluator versions, results, complexity, cost and the reason for every keep, discard or error. ([Original autoresearch](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch), [Experiment instructions](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch/blob/master/program.md))

Freeze scenarios, clock, expected results and thresholds before each batch; keep evaluator files outside the candidate’s editable scope. Require financial/inventory correctness, honest missingness/provenance, persistence, duplicate handling, accessible task completion and relevant build/regression checks before ranking usability. Measure task errors first, then founder completion time and steps; browser automation timing is only a proxy. Test an untouched holdout with different dates, variants and partners only at promotion, reviewed independently of implementation. Failed holdout cases become regressions; rotate new holdouts. Never tune expected answers to rescue a candidate or optimize the existing game rating as a substitute for business usefulness.

| Synthetic acceptance case | Required result |
|---|---|
| Dispatch 20 tees to a consignee; no reported sales yet | Brand-owned stock remains 20; consignee custody rises 20; sales, receivables and cash stay unchanged. |
| Eight $50 sales, one restocked return, 30% commission; receive $100 | Closing stock 13; consumer sales $350; proceeds $245; open settlement $145. Reimporting changes nothing. |
| Wholesale PO 24 × $30; invoice 18 units and allocate a $180 deposit | Invoice $540, balance $360, cash $180; six units/$180 remain an unshipped commitment. |
| Product has 40 units overall but zero medium available online | Identify the medium shortage and eligible locations; do not promise all 40 units. |
| Old statement imported today; sessions and costs missing | Preserve old coverage date; sessions/conversion and total margin remain unavailable. |

The founder judges brand fit, legibility and whether proposed actions help real work. A practical candidate hypothesis is “putting open balances and last statement coverage together reduces time to identify the next stockist follow-up without any reconciliation errors.” Real marketing or allocation experiments need their own budget and success criteria; public research cannot establish traffic volume, causal lift or a defensible advertising test sample size.

After the brand MD arrives, reconcile identity and vocabulary, reproduce the trust defects in isolation, and establish the initial evaluation baseline. Then implement validated durable records and agreed channel calculations, connect Claude’s read tools, and test the daily decision flow before refining visual hierarchy and gamification. The remaining operational inputs are compact: the current stockist/agreement roster, one representative wholesale and consignment record, an inventory/cost sample, actual Shopify capabilities, and preferred currency/timezone plus local or multi-device access.
